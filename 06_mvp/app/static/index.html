Lean-Month Planner

A concept for Groww's SIP screen. It helps first-time investors with irregular income get through a tight month without losing their SIP by accident.

Try a situation

How it works

  1. You describe your month in your own words, in English or Hinglish.
  2. AI understands the situation and when your money usually arrives.
  3. Rules, not AI, decide the options and their exact consequences.
  4. A quality gate checks every reply. If a check fails, you get a neutral fallback.
